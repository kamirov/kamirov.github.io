# Sequences Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026
Developer: Kamirova
Privacy and support contact: kamirova.apps@gmail.com

## Offline photo journal

Sequences is a general-purpose photo journal intended for ages 13 and above. No account is required. The app works offline and has no internet permission. It does not send your photos, sequence names, settings, or device data to the developer or third parties. There are no ads, analytics, tracking, or remote crash-reporting SDKs in the app.

## Camera and local data

Camera permission is used only to preview and capture photos when you use the camera. Sequences stores your photos and temporary captures, sequence names, capture dates and time-zone offsets, image dimensions, camera choices, ordering, overlay opacity, appearance, authentication setting, and acknowledgement of the device-storage notice locally. It does not request microphone, location, contacts, or photo-library access. Photos are not added to the system gallery.

## Device authentication

When authentication is enabled, Android checks your device PIN, pattern, password, or strong biometric. Sequences receives the authentication result, never your credentials or biometric templates. Changing the authentication setting requires a fresh device challenge.

## Storage and security

Photos and metadata are held in Android app-private storage. Backup and device-transfer rules exclude app data. Protection relies on Android's sandbox and device encryption, not custom photo encryption. Recent-apps previews are concealed. Screenshots and screen recordings remain allowed and can create copies outside Sequences. A compromised or rooted device is outside this protection.

## Retention and deletion

Saved photos and metadata remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall. Delete a photo from View, or delete an entire sequence from Edit. If private-file cleanup fails after deletion, restart the app to retry it. Abandoned temporary captures are removed at the next startup. The app does not provide forensic secure erasure.

There is no export, backup, recovery, or cloud copy. Uninstalling, clearing data, losing your device, or storage failure can permanently lose all photos. Deleting data inside Sequences does not delete screenshots or recordings made outside it.

## Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions, contact andrei.khramtsov@gmail.com. If you choose to email support using another app, that email and information you provide are handled by your email provider and the recipient to answer your request; do not include private photos. This policy describes Sequences itself. External services such as Google Play have their own privacy policies. Changes to the app's data practices will be reflected in an updated policy and effective date.
