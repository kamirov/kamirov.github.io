# Sequences Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Developer: Kami Rova

Privacy and support contact: kamirova.apps@gmail.com

## Photo journal with optional ad removal

Sequences is a general-purpose photo journal intended for ages 13 and above. No Sequences account is required. Capturing, storing, and viewing photos works offline. Photos, sequence names, reminders, and journal content remain on your device and are not sent to the developer, Google advertising services, or payment services. Advertising, privacy-choice management, purchases, and purchase restoration use an internet connection.

## Advertising and privacy choices

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display full-screen advertising after a photo is saved, when an ad is available. By default, it shows at most one ad per rolling 24 hours on an installation. Google may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers (and, where applicable, account-related identifiers), app interactions including ad views and taps, diagnostic information, and approximate location derived from IP addresses to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and, where allowed by your choices and applicable requirements, personalize advertising. Google receives ad requests, not your photographs or sequence content. Google's User Messaging Platform manages required privacy messages. When available, use Ad privacy choices in Settings to review or change your choices. Ad availability and personalization depend on those choices and your region. Google describes its practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Sequences does not add separate analytics or remote crash-reporting services.

## Purchases and restoration

A permanent, one-time Remove ads purchase is offered through Google Play, with pricing shown in your local currency. Google handles payment information; Sequences does not receive your card details. The app receives signed purchase records and stores an ad-removal receipt locally so removal continues offline. Restore purchases checks ownership with the Google Play account used to buy the product; it does not restore photos or journal data. Refunds or revoked ownership are reconciled when an online ownership check succeeds. Ad requests stop after ad removal is recognized. Google Play's own terms and privacy policies apply to payment processing.

## Camera and local data

Camera permission is used only to preview and capture photos when you use the camera. Sequences stores your photos and temporary captures, sequence names, capture dates and time-zone offsets, image dimensions, camera choices, ordering, overlay opacity, appearance, optional reminder schedules, authentication setting, and acknowledgement of the device-storage notice locally. It does not request microphone, location, contacts, or photo-library access. Photos are not added to the system gallery.

## Optional reminders

Each sequence can have no reminder or a daily, weekly, or monthly reminder. Android notification permission is requested when you first save an enabled reminder. Schedules stay on your device and work offline; boot and clock-change handling restores them after a restart or time-zone change. Android battery management may delay delivery. Notifications show the sequence name when unlocked and generic content on the lock screen, subject to your Android notification settings. Tapping a reminder opens its camera after app authentication when enabled. Disable reminders in the sequence editor or control delivery in Android notification settings.

## Device authentication

When authentication is enabled, Android checks your device PIN, pattern, password, or strong biometric. Sequences receives the authentication result, never your credentials or biometric templates. Changing the authentication setting requires a fresh device challenge.

## Storage and security

Photos and metadata are held in Android app-private storage. Backup and device-transfer rules exclude app data. Protection relies on Android's sandbox and device encryption, not custom photo encryption. Recent-apps previews are concealed. Screenshots and screen recordings remain allowed and can create copies outside Sequences. A compromised or rooted device is outside this protection.

## Retention and deletion

Saved photos and metadata remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall. Delete a photo from View, or delete an entire sequence from Edit. If private-file cleanup fails after deletion, restart the app to retry it. Abandoned temporary captures are removed at the next startup. The app does not provide forensic secure erasure.

There is no export, backup, recovery, or cloud copy. Uninstalling, clearing data, losing your device, or storage failure can permanently lose all photos. Deleting data inside Sequences does not delete screenshots or recordings made outside it.

## Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions, contact kamirova.apps@gmail.com. If you choose to email support using another app, that email and information you provide are handled by your email provider and the recipient to answer your request; do not include private photos. This policy describes Sequences itself. External services such as Google Play have their own privacy policies. Changes to the app's data practices will be reflected in an updated policy and effective date.

## Reading this policy in the app

Opening the privacy-policy reader downloads this document from GitHub’s hosting infrastructure. GitHub receives the network request, including your IP address and standard connection information; no photos or journal content are included. The app saves a local copy and its download time for offline reading and checks for an updated copy each time you open the reader. This copy is excluded from backup and device transfer and is removed when you clear app data or uninstall. Links you choose to open are handled by your browser and the destination website. GitHub describes its practices in its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
